Where should it run?

Everything happens on this device. Turning a site off switches off its tuned adapter — PromptBridge still works there, it just re-derives the page from structure. Any AI chat stays usable even with every hint disabled.

Voice and defaults

Both can be changed later from the panel or the library page.

Dictation tier

How you dictate

Dictate naturally — “budget 50K for tools, actually 75K” corrects itself, double-tap Alt+Shift+V to lock the mic on and say “send it”, and any word you retype gets remembered on this device. All of it is local; the learned vocabulary never leaves your browser.

Transfer fidelity

Privacy, in one paragraph

PromptBridge has no server. Packs live in this browser's storage. Style DNA stores counters — how long your prompts tend to be, whether you specify a format — never your text. Redaction scrubs emails, phone numbers, cards and API keys before anything leaves the page. The optional LLM tier only runs if you paste your own provider key, and that request goes straight from your browser to that provider.
